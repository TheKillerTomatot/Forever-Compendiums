A field guide to nine paths

00 / The opening hunt

A Hunter is not defined solely by a talent tab. The real build is a negotiated relationship between the bow, the beast, the ground beneath the target and the time available to fire.

The beast

Pet family changes the move set. Beast Mastery can invest in a permanent damage partner or hawks on target, but even two Beast Mastery characters need not maintain the same combat rhythm. [01] [07]

The weapon

Lone Wolf pays for a petless ranged specialist, whereas Survival invests in melee damage and moves between range brackets. Neither choice makes the 8-yard dead zone disappear. [01] [04] [18]

The terrain

Traps are usable midcombat but remain placed at the Hunter’s feet; all share a 30-second base cooldown. Their value depends as much on enemy movement as on damage. [06] [08]

The central answer. Two Hunters with the same specialization can play like different archetypes because Forever makes the active-pet state, pet-family signature, hawk/shot cooldown choice, melee-triggered Mongoose Bite, trap positioning and weapon range consequential. A pet-tank Warden, a hawk-focused Falconer and a Bestial Wrath Commander each solve the same pull differently – without changing their class. [01] [04] [07]

01 / Anatomy of the Hunter

A good Hunter rotation is a living diagram of threat, position, weapon availability, and future cooldowns – not a memorized list of damage buttons.

SYSTEM I
♟

The companion

One permanent active pet may tank, bleed, disarm, root, debuff or support a group according to family. It inherits Hunter stats and needs training, healing, happiness and deliberate commands.

Pets received a live 115%-health correction. Their threat matters only when Growl, pathing and target selection are managed. [03] [07] [11]

SYSTEM II
⌖

The firing lane

Bow, crossbow or gun plus ammunition. Ranged shots cannot be used inside the 8-yard dead zone. Auto Shot requires 0.5 seconds stationary and continues through Aimed or Sniper casts in the October rules.

Aimed Shot shares with Multi-Shot; Arcane Shot shares with Summon Hawk. [08] [09] [18]

The Hunter spends mana, while the permanent pet spends its own Focus. Stings are mutually exclusive per Hunter/target. Traps go under your feet, may be used in combat and share a 30-second cooldown. [04] [08]

The pet selection decision tree

Taking repeated hits?

Choose Turtle’s Shell Shield, Bear’s Swipe or Boar’s Charge. Growl on for solo; pet should engage before the hunter commits.

Need target control?

Spider Web root, Bird of Prey Mine! disarm, Crab Pinch slow or Bat Sonic Blast caster slow. Pet on passive near a Freezing Trap.

Group contribution?

Wolf Furious Howl aids melee damage; Tallstrider Dust Cloud reduces armor; Carrion Bird Screech reduces attack power. Select what the group lacks.

Target unreachable by pet?

Expect reduced pet value; decide if an independent Lone Wolf ranged build fits instead. Do not assume a family can bypass all encounter mechanics.

Aspect and sting ledger

One active Aspect: Hawk enhances ranged AP, Beast enhances melee AP, Monkey helps dodge, Cheetah/Pack adds movement with daze risk, Wild adds Nature resistance. One Sting per Hunter on a target: Serpent for ticking Nature damage, Scorpid to reduce target hit by 2%, Viper to drain mana from eligible targets. Improved Stings modifies those three differently. Ammo, weapons, and consumables

The live-era Hunter still uses arrows or bullets and a quiver/ammo pouch; sourced Wowhead reporting sees no notable ammo-system replacement. Stock mana consumables, food, bandages and appropriate pet food. Maintain melee weapon skills if taking Survival; no verified Forever-specific universal raid consumable order or fixed ammo quiver exception is asserted. [06] [10]

The petless damage exchange

With active pet

Two health bars, two attackers

The permanent pet can draw threat, keep attacking during brief Hunter movement, deliver Wolf Howl or Web and absorb hits. But it needs food, training, pathing, health and boss safety. Focused Fire activates. Lone Wolf does not. [04] [07] [14]

Without active pet

One character, stronger shots

Lone Wolf gives +20% damage to the Hunter’s attacks only without an active pet. No separate pet damage, no pet Growl, no signature ability, no pet Focused Fire. You own every moment of threat, survival and range recovery. [04] [05] [14]

The long-range firing sequence

01Start ≥8 yards from target; Hunter’s Mark / Sting selection.
02Stand 0.5 s so Auto Shot can fire; avoid late reposition.
03Sniper Shot at 45 yd if talented; opens +10 yd on next three shots in 10 s.
04Choose Aimed Shot on single enemy or Multi-Shot on multiple – shared cooldown.
05Continue Auto Shot through compatible long casts; move when mechanics require it.

Illustrative priorities, not fixed cast timestamps, DPS proof or promise of three full shots within every Sniper range window. Aimed Shot is a trained ability from level 20 in Forever; Sniper Shot remains a Marksmanship capstone. [01] [04] [06] [09]

Strider Kick is 100% melee weapon damage and +30% movement for three seconds; it is a strike with an escape/reposition component, not a permanent sprint. Predator’s Edge improves melee ability critical damage plus off-hand weapon damage, not unconditional polearm damage. Survivalist’s Discipline cuts the cooldown of Traps and Deterrence by 20/40%, not fire damage. All effects are based on Forever talent descriptions. [04] [05] [06]

What makes melee genuinely supported?

Expose Prey solves part of the old dodge-dependency problem by allowing marked-target attacks to enable Mongoose Bite. Lacerating adds a real bleed; Aspect of the Beast now supplies melee AP; Strider Kick, Resourcefulness and Predator’s Edge support the cycle. But the client’s Lacerating replacement rule makes blind refreshing inefficient. [04] [06] [08]

When ranged remains preferable

Constant boss cleaves, forced separation, dangerous ground hazards, low melee uptime and ranged mechanics favor staying outside melee. The Hunter's pet cannot guarantee the player’s melee survival. No cited Forever combat data establishes spear DPS greater than a well-equipped bow on every raid encounter. [01] [06] [15]

Pet apprenticeship

Tame, train, feed

Complete the level-10 Hunter class quest. Never try to tame a creature above your level in Forever. When learning ranks through a teacher beast, stable main pet → tame teacher → fight until learned → return → Beast Training. Pet happiness still affects damage and must be maintained. [07] [11]

Weapons & ammo

Carry both systems

Keep appropriate arrows/bullets and an ammo bag or quiver. Damage play requires a functioning ranged weapon even for hybrid Survival pulls; strong melee weapons and trained weapon skills matter for Spear Stalker. Forever weapon speed differences and pet attack speeds warrant live tests rather than imported spreadsheet breakpoints. [06] [10]

Class & race choices

Racials, not folklore

Orc Command is now +5% pet damage; Dwarf Artisan’s Precision is +1% hit; Human Ruthless is +1% crit after Oct 26. Other races trade movement, survivability or resource effects. Avoid declaring a best racial from old Classic data; compare the actual player’s objective. [06] [09]

Pet-tanking and petless leveling are not equivalent

Pet tank loop

01Feed and set Growl
02Pet takes threat
03Hunter shoots safely
04Mend if needed
05Recover and repeat

A good pet reduces damage taken by the Hunter but costs mana, training and food. A dead pet may cause sudden downtime. Spirit Bond and Improved Mend Pet are valuable for difficult solo chains. [04] [07]

Petless ranged loop

01Mark and find range
02Personal bonus active
03Enemy closes
04Slow / Scatter / trap
05Replant, recover

Fewer animal upkeep decisions, but more active kiting and potentially more bandage/food downtime against dangerous enemies. Lone Wolf is attractive when pet contact would be poor; not automatically an easier solo leveler. [04] [14]

Raid optimization without counterfeit math

Weapon DPS, hit chance, Agility, attack power, crit, mana/Intellect and pet survivability all matter – but the specific ordering changes with build and encounter. Forever’s hit cap against each level difference is not established by the examined primary sources; do not assume Classic’s 9%. Maintain Trueshot Aura where appropriate, position pets behind frontal attacks, and avoid freezing a target the group intends to cleave. [04] [06] [08] [10]

PvP is about space and diminishing returns

Concussive, Wing Clip, Scatter, traps, Intimidation, pet Web/Mine! and Strider Kick create different movement games. Stuns, roots and disorients may overlap or be diminished; a trap goes under your feet rather than on a remote enemy. The target’s movement skill matters more than a blanket DPS build label. [06] [08] [09]

Consumables and class quests. The level-10 taming quest is documented; further Hunter class quest rewards, exact potion rankings and ammo-bag future changes are not sufficiently verified for this edition. Stock relevant ammo, ordinary mana and health supplies, food, bandages and family-appropriate pet food. Confirm current vendor prices, raid consumable rules and profession-specific bonuses in your client. [06] [07] [10]

Client-confirmed

Lone Wolf

The 1-rank Marksmanship talent increases the Hunter’s attacks by 20% while no pet is active. The wording does not assert twenty percent more combined hunter-and-pet damage. Petless and active-pet Focused Fire cannot both be active. [04] [05] [14]

Client-confirmed

Summon Hawk

One rank, 15-point Beast Mastery tier. Targeted, 18-second hawk attacker, maximum two; the initial hit cites 32 + 5% ranged AP. Summon Hawk and Arcane Shot share their cooldown; it is not an independent permanent tame. [04] [06] [12]

Client-confirmed

Survival melee chain

Hunter’s Mark enables Expose Prey’s Mongoose Bite activation window; Lacerating Strikes adds a 21-second bleed based on actual Mongoose Bite damage. This makes melee supported, not automatically best in every raid. [04] [05] [09]

Research discipline. Do not interpret the old +150% pet health bug as intended pet-tanking strength; do not count Arcane Shot and hawks as independent cooldowns; do not claim all traps recharge separately; do not assign a universal 9% hit cap borrowed from Classic. Sources disagree about certain pet family counts and some live pet-skill persistence. Those uncertainties remain disclosed in Chapter 10. [03] [06] [07]

Spider Web to create an extra exit angle, wolf for incidental melee group support. Pet helps cover the dead zone, but the player’s weapon-swapping and Aspect swapping create the identity. Pet does not eliminate trap positioning. [07] [11]

Weapon choice and equipment

Balanced melee and ranged weapon quality rather than overoptimizing one. Keep weapon DPS, Agility, hit and mana in mind, plus Stamina for time spent close. A 2H spear forfeits Predator’s Edge offhand benefit; dual-wield uses it but may have different weapon-quality tradeoffs. Weapon-speed note: Both melee swing frequency and ranged Auto Shot planting matter; test real weapon pairs through the 8-yard switch rather than assume weaving is always profitable. [08] [10]

Opening play sequence

Hunter’s Mark while ranged → Serpent Sting if long pull → Auto Shot whenever stationary ≥0.5s → shoot to fish Expose Prey → use Concussive Shot to keep range → when enemy gets inside 8 yards, switch Aspect and melee auto/Raptor/Mongoose Bite → Strider Kick for exit → replant ranged.

Damage over time & ongoing decisions

Treat range changes as a decision tree, not a set rotation. Multi-Shot and Aimed Shot share a cooldown; late Aimed casts risk being caught in the dead zone. Move with the boss, use instant controls, then resume Auto Shot instead of seeking impossible continuous firing.

Burst cadence and cooldowns

Short melee burst is a tactical response, not necessarily higher total boss output. Rapid Killing’s next-shot effect after eligible kills is useful in chains; Resourcefulness supports mixed melee/ranged crit mana windows.

Mobility, defense, trap response

Deterrence and Counterattack buy time at contact; Clever Traps, pet Web, Wing Clip and Strider Kick form a layered escape route. Attack switching can fail near 8 yards, so use clear movement discipline. [08] [09]

Mana, ammo and consumable pressure

Carry arrows/bullets and have trained melee weapon skills. Aspect of the Hawk for ranged AP vs Aspect of the Beast for melee AP are mutually exclusive; constant swapping has action/time cost. No petless benefit.

The lesson. The most important difference is not “which button hits hardest?” It is what happens on the step when the plan breaks: a pet-focused Commander repositions an ally, a Lone Sniper must reclaim space alone, and a Trapmaster converts enemy movement into a defensive resource. [04] [07] [08]
