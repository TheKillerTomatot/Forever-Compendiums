02 / The Priest’s foundation

Four ways to spend the same second

A Priest has health to restore, incoming damage to prevent, mana to preserve, and enemies to disrupt. The priority is dictated by the next failure—not by a fixed damage-style rotation.

I. Forecast

What will happen? A shield is valuable only if it catches actual damage before expiration. Ask who will receive the next strike, whether they already have Weakened Soul, and whether shielding early would make a later hit unwardable.

II. Treat

How soon must recovery land? Greater Heal wins on large, predictable wounds if it will finish in time; Flash Heal wins on imminent lethal damage. Overhealing consumes Mana and may keep Spirit regeneration suppressed.

III. Account

What is the second cast costing? Spell ranks, the five-second rule, Meditation, Spirit Tap and MP5 determine long-fight efficiency. Litany makes genuine name changes beneficial; gratuitous spell variation is not free.

IV. Interfere

Which enemy action can be prevented? Fear Ward, Dispel Magic, Psychic Scream, Mana Burn, Shadow Silence, race-specific abilities and movement can outperform a heal in the right setting. Constraints differ in Shadowform.

Penance has two targets and one shared cooldown

Healing target

On a friendly target: an instant pulse plus one pulse each second for two seconds. Use when the next several seconds of predicted healing are valuable, especially if the target has Weakened Soul and you invested Renewed Hope.

It is a channel, not an invulnerable instant spell. Movement or an interruption can truncate later pulses.

Enemy target

On an enemy: Holy damage in the same 2-second channel. Power in Light enhances damage after your Holy Fire or Shadow Word: Pain. A Battle Confessor might use it offensively to shorten the fight; an Aegis Keeper should preserve it for a fatal incoming tank hit.

Using it offensively relinquishes its defensive use during the 12-second cooldown.

Damage or healing? Ask: (1) Is the ally safe until Penance returns? (2) Will a kill or interrupt substitute for healing? (3) Is the enemy properly debuffed for Power in Light? (4) Can you stand still for the whole channel? If any answer is no, avoid a speculative offensive channel. [B2] [T1] [D2]

Divine Aegis is not another Power Word: Shield

Aegis comes after a non-periodic critical heal, absorbs a share of that healing for 12 seconds, and does not stack with itself. You cannot guarantee a crit when a tank needs one. Its listed cap is disputed among current client-data sites: 1,200 in some tooltip mirrors, 3,600 in another export. No exact cap is used in the build ratings or diagrams.

[B1] [D1] [T1]

Weakened Soul as resource

The base 15-second target lockout means unlimited Shield casts are not unlimited shields on the same patient. Renewed Hope at 5/5 subtracts 5 seconds from the remaining Weakened Soul when a listed direct heal actually heals that debuffed target; it also increases that heal’s crit chance by 10% on that target.

Rank 4 Soulward Medic instead obtains 4% crit / 4-second shortening. Soul Warding has Improved Shield 3/3 as a real prerequisite. [T1] [T2]

Litany is a reward, not a command

At 2/2, Litany refunds 10% of the BASE Mana cost of a healing spell if the previous heal cast had a different name. Heal → Renew qualifies as two names; Heal → Heal does not. It is usually wrong to cast unwanted Renew only to earn a fraction of its cost back.

Watch heal spell identity and resource expense, rather than demanding a fixed “rotation.” [T1] [D4]

Small, fast, large: which is efficient?

Heal: modest, relatively efficient damage recovery. Flash Heal: faster and useful when a dying ally cannot wait, potentially adding Holy Binding self-healing. Greater Heal: large delayed restoration; Divine Fury shortens it by 0.5 s at 5/5 and Improved Healing reduces the Mana cost of Heal/GH and other listed spells by up to 15%.

At high spell power a downrank may be attractive, but Forever’s changed coefficients and real overheal must be tested. [T1] [D2]

Four concrete Holy triage verdicts

Tank loses 10% health slowly
Use affordable Heal or wait. Overfilling with Greater Heal is worse than canceling the cast.
Seraph aims reliable timing; Choir waits to spend group Mana until the group is actually hurt.
Tank falls dangerously low
Flash Heal now or other instant safety, then a well-timed larger cast.
Holy Binding only self-heals when you Flash Heal another target; Prayer of Mending may also trigger on direct healing.
Four of five allies are hurt
Prayer of Healing on that party when within range; Mending seeks nearby hops.
Different player groups require different targets. A split raid is not a single five-person healing circle.
Sustained movement / interrupt pressure
Preplace Renew / Mending, dodge first, then Flash Heal in the safe cast window.
Twilight Focus helps pushback, not every interrupt or crowd-control effect.
Spiritual Guidance and the hybrid trap: at 5/5, Spirit contributes up to 25% to spell healing but only ~8% to spell damage. Searing Light improves Holy damage by 5% at 2/2 and enables a chance for free Holy Nova from Holy Fire periodic damage. Mind Flay is not just a slow

Mind Flay is a three-second Shadow channel with a 50% movement slow. Improved Mind Flay 2/2 makes each successive damage instance 20% stronger than the previous. With this talent, interrupting before the final tick disproportionately weakens the channel. Dark Inspiration gives the party +15% Spirit for 10 seconds after Mind Blast or a fully completed Mind Flay at 5/5. A clipped channel is not the full-channel trigger. [T1] [D3]

Vampiric Embrace: the correct beneficiaries

Vampiric Embrace marks a target for 30 seconds; your Shadow spell damage causes party members to receive healing equal to 20% of that damage. Its heal cannot critically strike since the November change. It was made unresistable; it does not become a raid-wide healing aura. A dead VE target can also satisfy Spirit Tap’s described trigger. Signature synergies & essential abilities

Improved Shield → Soul Warding (four seconds off shield cooldown, −15% cost) → Weakened Soul → Renewed Hope-enabled crit healing → Divine Aegis after a non-periodic critical heal. Power Infusion is donated to the healer or caster with the largest immediate benefit.

Healing, warding or damage decision priorities

Predict the next real hit; shield with enough lead time to absorb it but not so early that the 30-second shield expires. Penance on a threatened ally, then correctly sized Heal / Greater Heal; use Flash Heal only when delay would kill. Renew on reliable future damage; Penance can damage when the health plan is secure.

Offensive and defensive cooldowns

Power Infusion for agreed burst; Inner Focus on an expensive cast with useful crit potential; Penance every 12 s when a target warrants its three pulses. Fear Ward is baseline; Psychic Scream is an escape, never an uncontrolled dungeon pull.

Mana plan, spell ranks & efficiency

Meditation 3/3 (50% Spirit regeneration while casting), Mental Agility 3/3, Soul Warding discount and appropriate Heal ranks. Do not re-shield healthy players merely because cooldown is free.

Gear, consumables & important stats

Healing power, Spirit, Intellect, MP5 and survival. Crit has special value from Divine Aegis but its absorb is bounded, nonstacking and cannot be demanded on schedule.

Positioning, threat & survival

Far enough behind tank for clean line of sight; stand clear of cleaves. Preserve a second safe angle for Penance’s two-second channel.

Group support, dispels & emergency tools

Primary tank damage planner; counters telegraphed strikes, offers Power Infusion and dispels. Against Warrior tanks, Forever says absorbs do not remove their damage-taken Rage.

Solo, dungeon, raid and PvP applications

Solo: wand + Shadow Word: Pain and defensive shields; Dungeon: pre-hit ward; Raid: assigned tank windows; PvP: saves teammates, but has limited direct disruption.

Strengths, weaknesses & sacrificed options

Foregoes Holy Binding, Litany and Prayer of Mending for deeper Discipline throughput and Power Infusion.

Sacred or shadow oath: Prevention first; direct healing is the second layer, not an admission of failure.

What your equipment actually changes

For protection and healing

  • Bonus healing: increases the work of correctly timed healing, not wasted overheal.
  • Spirit: supports regeneration and Holy Spiritual Guidance; some racial mana recovery also scales with Spirit.

For Shadow and offensive Holy

  • Shadow spell power: real periodic and direct damage; Vampiric Embrace party healing is a function of Shadow spell damage actually dealt.

  • PvP gear: prioritize stamina, control escape tools and enough spell hit for reliable disruption, not invented Retail-style resilience ratings.

How to verify one claim yourself

  1. Check the build ID and date on a source against the live client.
  2. Equip a known set of items; inspect the actual spell tooltip and ranks.
  3. For heals, record incoming damage, effective healing and overheal, then repeat.

The closing revelation

Different Priests within one specialization make different promises. The Aegis Keeper promises a hit will be less dangerous; the Battle Confessor promises a dangerous enemy may die earlier; the Soulward Medic promises a near-death patient can recover in time. The Holy trio measures wounds by their timing and distribution. The Shadow trio measures damage against Mana, group sustain and the power to deny an enemy cast.

That is why the correct build is more than a 51-point string: it is a decision-making contract with the group.
