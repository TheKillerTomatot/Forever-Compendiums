00 / The Opening Blade

Victory begins before the first blow.

A Warlock reshapes the field with curses and companions. A Rogue reshapes time: the time an enemy cannot act, the time a second target stays Sapped, the time until Energy arrives, and the brief time a poisoned dagger has to matter.

The first question
01

What survives the opener?

Cheap Shot or Ambush can win a short pull. But if an elite lives, a talented Rogue needs a second answer: steady Energy generation, Slice and Dice, a safe finisher or a reset.

The second question
02

How does steel become damage?

Weapons are not interchangeable decorations. Mutilate requires both daggers; Hack and Slash changes behavior with swords, axes, maces, daggers and fists; off-hand speed affects poison opportunities.

The third question
03

Who owns the next six seconds?

Venom triggers for six seconds after Shank; Death Mark banks damage for six seconds; Ghostly Strike opens a seven-second no-Stealth Ambush/Garrote window. The clock dictates priorities. Evidence ledger ↗

The central finding. Two Rogues of the same tree can fight in entirely different rhythms: sustained weapon attackers optimize melee uptime, poison assassins optimize poison triggers, dagger executioners compress combo-point production, and stealth operatives trade raw damage talents for control over the encounter's opening and reset.

02 / Anatomy · Resources and control

The assassination is an economy.

Energy is continuously regained, combo points are attached to a chosen target, and the stealth advantage is spent as soon as you engage. Good Rogues plan those three economies at once. [O1] [G2]

Resource 01
100

Energy maximum

Baseline 100 Energy, or up to 110 with Vigor 2/2. Smooth recovery is approximately 10 Energy/sec before special effects. Adrenaline Rush doubles regeneration for 15 seconds.

Resource 02
5

Combo-point cap

Builders generate target-specific combo points; finishers consume them. Mutilate adds two; Premeditation adds two; Cheap Shot adds two; Boot Knife can make Shank add two. Crit bonuses are conditional.

Poised reduces the next Energy ability by 10 after reaching full Energy out of combat. Stealth access, rear position and the enemy's control status are nonrenewable advantages until a reset works.

Poison chemistry

Instant is direct proc damage; Deadly rewards elapsed time and repeated applications; Wound reduces healing; Crippling slows; Mind-numbing interferes with casting. Forever gives poisons more charges, coexistence with stones, and Attack Power scaling on Instant/Deadly with doubled coefficients in November. [O1] [O2] [G3]

Each weapon's speed, hit events and poison placement matter. Dual Wield Specialization helps off-hand physical hits—not a blanket statement that each poison scales identically with weapon speed.

Stealth and target management

Sap works on eligible out-of-combat Humanoids and now preserves Stealth. Vanish re-enters improved Stealth and breaks movement impairments but is a cooldown—not guaranteed immunity to incoming damage. Redirect in client spell data moves stored combo points to the next non-player target hit within its stated window.

Bleeds and damaging poisons can break Blind and frustrate a re-Sap. Plan crowd control before applying damage-over-time effects on a future CC target.

Can you open from Stealth?Yes → Select opener

Rear dagger: Ambush. Need stun: Cheap Shot. Long living target: Garrote. Premeditation must be committed before its CP expire.

Did the opener fail?Yes → Spend the backup

Sinister Strike, Mutilate or Hemorrhage from a safe side. Ghostly Strike can enable Ambush/Garrote without Stealth, but positioning still matters.

Will the target outlive five CP?Yes → Maintain a cycle

Slice and Dice and high-value Rupture may outweigh one early Eviscerate; use cooldowns only when you can attack through their window.

03 / Assassination · Poisoner's Laboratory

Three ways to make one cut fatal.

Assassination is not simply an instruction to use daggers. Its tension is between poison throughput, critical-strike-driven combo points, and spending talent points on the control needed to deliver a final blow.

Venom Alchemist

Repeated chemistry. Improved Poisons 5/5, Vile Poisons 5/5, Shank → Venom and strong refund talent investment. On a long enemy, repeated poison events and maintained finishers matter more than one crit.

Test: venom uptime, damage split, poison charge use, CP waste.

Dagger Executioner

Fewer builders. Mutilate's two-CP baseline plus Seal Fate and Puncturing Wounds changes how often a finisher becomes available. The target must be poisoned, both daggers equipped, and Energy conserved.

Test: Mutilate crit frequency, finisher cadence, positional failure.

Cold-Blooded Duelist

A controlled verdict. Invests in Premeditation, Initiative, Hemorrhage and Dirty Deeds instead of Mutilate. Cheap Shot can supply an immediate five CP with preloaded Premeditation and max Initiative.

Test: success after trinket, duration of control, kill window.

04 / Combat · Blademaster's Arsenal

Weapons write the rotation.

Combat rewards time on target. It also changes its output according to the chosen weapon type: Hack and Slash folds the old separate weapon talents into one flexible rule, not a universal sword advantage.

Swordmaster

Slow main-hand sword or axe → reliable Sinister Strike and extra-attack opportunities; 15 Assassination points support Slice and Dice and crit. Commitment: consistent damage over the whole fight.

Blade Dancer

Five Flash of Steel ranks → stronger Shank Energy engine; Blade Flurry + Adrenaline Rush in a 15-sec window. Commitment: two enemies truly standing together.

Tactical Duelist

Interrupts, Riposte, Improved Sprint, Premeditation and reduced Energy costs. Gives up Adrenaline Rush for control. Commitment: decisive responses to dangerous mechanics.

Hack and Slash: three actual weapon incentives

⚔

Swords / axes

At 5/5, successful melee weapon attacks have a 5% chance to trigger one extra attack on the target. Extra attacks are a weapon-specific proc, not guaranteed per strike.

✧

Daggers / fists

At 5/5, increases critical-strike chance by 5%. Best understood alongside attack choice and crit-sensitive talents, not a license to Mutilate without two daggers.

◇

Maces

At 5/5, attacks ignore 15% of target armor. It is no longer the old mace stun proc; target armor and your other armor effects matter.

The 5-rank talent offers distinct weapon-dependent benefits. Mix-and-match weapon conditions and proc behavior should be checked against actual equipped weapons and logs. [D1] [D4]

Fight-length engineering

Ten-second trash

If the foe will die before a long buff pays back, spending multiple builders to establish Slice and Dice can be inferior to a direct Eviscerate. Shank may save enough Energy for one more attack; Blade Flurry might be wasted when only one foe is present.

Ninety-second boss

Slice and Dice uptime, avoiding energy-cap waste, stable weapon contact, accurate swings and using cooldowns in uninterrupted melee become dominant decisions. A sword or axe user can keep attacking from the front; a rear-position dagger opener is optional rather than the entire loop.

05 / Subtlety · Shadow Operative's Codex

Stealth is a method, not a permanent state.

Subtlety in live Forever ends in Death Mark—not Shadow Dance or Thousand Cuts. Its distinctiveness is the way it converts temporary access, a Ghostly Strike bridge and controlled target selection into more chances to act.

Ambush Predator

Rear dagger → Improved Ambush, Premeditation, Initiative and 5/5 Boot Knife. Wants a fight so short or a reset so reliable that its opener remains the story.

Shadow Controller

Quick Getaway and Dirty Tricks give meaningful multi-Sap routing, while Elusiveness, Preparation and improved defenses offer a second plan.

Persistent Infiltrator

Hemorrhage, Serrated Blades, Blood Splatter and Death Mark make an actual post-opener bleed-and-finisher loop; its Boot Knife is low rank, so CP efficiency is weaker.

The seven-second exception: Ghostly Strike

Ghostly Strike is 40 Energy, 20-second cooldown. With main-hand dagger its listed weapon-damage percentage is higher. It removes the next Ambush/Garrote Stealth requirement, but the client text does not explicitly lift the separate rear/dagger requirements. [D1] [D4]

What Death Mark actually offers

At 25 Energy and a 10-sec cooldown, Death Mark is a finisher that stores a portion of your damage per combo point, counting Garrote/Rupture at twice the storage rate, then releases stored damage after six seconds. When a powerful opener goes wrong

Target sees through Stealth? Open with Hemorrhage or Sinister Strike. Opponent turns? Ambush needs a rear angle; Ghostly Strike enables no-Stealth use but positioning still matters. Enemy survives? Keep enough Energy to control or finish. Cannot safely Vanish? Use Evasion, Sprint, Gouge, Blind and the sustained engine; do not narrate an imaginary guaranteed re-open.

Preparation is a powerful 10-minute reset cooldown and not a continuously repeatable rotation. A bleed that breaks Blind can defeat your own control strategy.

Rear access + dagger?Yes → Ambush

Opportunity and Improved Ambush support the first hit. Premeditation and Initiative accelerate CP; spend before Boot Knife overcaps.

Enemy survives and turns?Yes → Hemorrhage / Ghostly

Front-safe Hemorrhage is the bridge to Rupture; Ghostly can allow another no-Stealth opener if its prerequisites still hold.

Need to leave or CC?Yes → Blind / Vanish / Preparation

Watch DoTs, immunities, trinkets, diminishing returns and enemies already in combat before attempting a reset.

II / Weapons & speed

Two daggers; inspect fast off-hand poison attempts versus off-hand damage, keep a main-hand dagger with strong damage for Mutilate. Test speed instead of assuming faster always wins.

III / Poison protocol

Deadly Poison on the frequently striking hand; Instant Poison on the other for direct procs. Swap to Wound or Crippling when target healing/escape matters.

IV / Builders & combo points

Mutilate (2 CP, 60 Energy, dual daggers) after a poison lands; Shank for Energy and Venom, not baseline combo points; Sinister Strike only when awkward positioning/weapon conditions demand it.

V / Finishers & their timing

Refresh Slice and Dice before it drops; maintain Rupture only when its duration will play out; Eviscerate for a dying target. Improved Expose Armor is a group-assigned alternative, not a free personal DPS gain.

VI / The opening sequence

Stealth → Garrote from behind against a lasting target → Shank once there is room for its Energy return → Mutilate on a poisoned target → build to a useful Slice and Dice or Rupture.

VII / Energy and the next action

Vigor 2/2 allows 110 maximum Energy. Full Relentless Strikes refunds 25 Energy from a 5-point finisher by tooltip probability, but never budget the refund as guaranteed. Avoid Shank at cap.

VIII / Offensive cooldowns

Cold Blood should target an eligible direct attack or Eviscerate; Shank every ≈10 seconds when its poison window and refund can be used. No Adrenaline Rush, no Preparation.

IX / Control & defense

Gouge, Kick, Blind and Vanish remain tools, but this point split offers little Subtlety reset control. Choose one safe single target; never let poison ticks break a planned Blind.

X / Positioning & movement

Garrote requires Stealth and rear access without Dirty Deeds. Mutilate does not require the rear but demands daggers in both hands; use a short route behind for the opener only when safe.

XI / Dungeon / raid suitability

Strong investigation target for durable single targets, particularly when all poison/bleed ticks finish; not established as top DPS.

XII / PvP suitability

Playable but dispels, immunity and fast burst windows undermine the investment. Wound/Crippling concessions cut poison output.

XIII / Levels 1–60

Train Shank at 24, Venom after 20 Assassination points, Mutilate at 31 Assassination points (typically level 40). Until then use Sinister Strike/Backstab and finishers.

XIV / Stats & equipment

Weapon quality → hit reliability → Agility/AP and critical strike; evaluate poison application rate, target armor, debuff slots and uptime. Fast off-hand is a testing hypothesis.

XV / Strengths

Poison uptime, finisher recycling and long-bleed value; its resource loop evolves after Shank.

XVI / Weaknesses

Burst is conditional; Mutilate locks two daggers; mobile targets waste bleeds; much of its payoff can vanish with immunity or short fights.

XVII / Cost of the choices

41 points in Assassination means no Blade Flurry, Adrenaline Rush, Preparation or Boot Knife.

XVIII / Same-specialization contrast

Against Dagger Executioner, poison talents buy more repeated applications while sacrificing some critical Mutilate payoff from Combat.

Live resource ledger / change the sequence

A conservative illustrative accounting model shows how Poised, builder costs, Shank, Combo Points and optional waits affect the first several actions. This is intentionally not a damage simulator: it assumes successful hits, no critical procs and no weapon swings, treats nonmax-rank Boot Knife as zero guaranteed CP, and uses a configurable interval between actions.

Model: begin at race-neutral full Energy (100 or 110 with Vigor; Gnome and other racial effects excluded), apply Poised once to the first Energy-costing button, spend the listed ability cost, add Shank's return, and recover 10 Energy/sec between actions. Wait to afford an ability if needed. Track CP to five and spend on finishers.
Not modeled: attacks missing, cooldown availability, Energy overflow between server steps, autoattacks, poison ticks, proc chances, crowd-control resistance, target switching, energy from raid buffs, actual GCD timing or player reaction. Therefore displayed timestamps are not predictions.

Three combat phases, three distinct priorities

Assassination / elite

  1. Venom Alchemist: choose a lasting poison/bleed window.
  2. Dagger Executioner: convert crit-assisted Mutilate into timely finishers.
  3. Cold-Blooded Duelist: reserve control and deliver a crit when it can close the fight.

Combat / four enemies

  1. Swordmaster: split pull and keep a stable target.
  2. Blade Dancer: align two targets for Flurry and Shank.
  3. Tactical Duelist: Sap / Blind / Kick priority targets and survive the pull.

Subtlety / PvP

  1. Ambush Predator: force a reaction to the first hit.
  2. Shadow Controller: trade cooldowns and control until opponent runs out.
  3. Persistent Infiltrator: pressure through a failed opening with Hemo / bleeds, while protecting a reset.
08 / Leveling, Weapons, PvE & PvP

A Rogue becomes dangerous in stages.

Talent choices do not compensate for a poor weapon indefinitely. From one to sixty, prioritize trained ranks, a current main-hand weapon, sufficient hit consistency, reliable escapes and the ability to avoid unnecessary fights.

Weapons first

Sinister Strike scales with your main-hand attack; dagger talents only pay off when the right weapons are equipped. Mutilate explicitly needs two daggers. For Combat, a new high-DPS sword/axe/mace can be better than defending a low-DPS preferred weapon just for Hack and Slash type.

Leveling with fewer bandages

Sap, Pick Pocket, stealth routing and single pulls reduce health loss. Gouge, Evasion and Sprint can convert unsafe encounters into manageable ones. First Aid and consumables matter because Rogues lack a dedicated self-healing rotation.

Groups bring utility

Kick interrupts, Sap controls eligible Humanoids, Blind buys time, Feint drops threat, Expose Armor can serve the group and Disarm Trap/Pick Lock add access. Ask the tank before overwriting a group armor plan. [O1] [O2] [G3]

Races, factions and customized identity

Racials are tactical, not a universal DPS mandate

Forever revised many racial passives and actives after October feedback. Humans have Will to Survive for stun removal and Ruthless for crit rather than the old sword-skill passive. Orc axes no longer mean the old mandatory weapon-skill outcome. Evaluate the actual racial for your build, faction rules, fight conditions and personal preference; don't import Classic racial rankings. [O2] [D1]

Dual specialization at 40

Use a practical second loadout: Combat for long dungeon weapon damage plus Subtlety for PvP control, or Assassination daggers for boss pressure plus a safer questing build. Remember that the second spec doesn't supply weapons, poisons or mana; it cannot make a missing pair of daggers appear. Respecs and Legacy talents use class trainers. [O1] [D4]

PvE and PvP measure different success. PvE asks whether you can stand in melee and spend CP efficiently; PvP asks whether the opponent gets to act at all, whether a trinket breaks your chain, and whether you can control distance after the opener.
